BLO VAULT Support

Get help

  • In the app, open More and tap Send feedback to report a problem or ask a question.
  • Replies to your reports appear in More → Message Center.
  • Or email info@blovault.com.

Manage or cancel your subscription

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose BLO VAULT. You can change or cancel there; cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next renewal. Refunds follow Apple's rules.

Restore purchases

If you already subscribed and the app shows the free trial, open More, find the plan card at the top and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

What happens when the trial ends

New accounts get a 14-day free trial with up to 3 linked institutions. When the trial ends without an active subscription, syncing stops and we remove your bank connections at Plaid. Data already on your device stays until you delete it, and you can link again after subscribing. BLO Pro is $5.99/month for up to 10 institutions. Apple Card and Apple Cash connected through Apple's FinanceKit are not counted.

Delete your account

In the app open More → Settings, then under Danger zone tap Delete account permanently and follow the prompts. Your data on the phone is erased immediately, and we remove your bank connections and delete your account by the last day of that calendar month. Deleting the app alone does not disconnect your banks or delete your account.
