How it works

  1. Connect your accounts.

    Apple Card and Apple Cash connect through Apple on your iPhone with "Add Apple Card", with no bank login (iOS 17.4 or later). Other banks and cards connect read-only through Plaid. Many US banks and cards are available through Plaid; some institutions aren't.

  2. See what's left until payday.

    Cash Flow starts with today's bank balance and places each bill, card payment and paycheck on its date.

  3. Get a heads-up before checking dips below zero.

    When the app sees a payment that could put checking below zero, it schedules a notification up to three days ahead. It's an estimate, updated each time you open the app. You decide what to move or wait on.

Also in the app

Recurring and subscriptions. Bills and subscriptions in one list. When a subscription's price goes up, Recurring shows a note.

Card spending by the day you used it. In your transaction list, card purchases show on the day you used the card, when your bank reports that date.

Monthly recap. In the first 10 days of each month, Home shows last month's money in, money out and one category that ran above usual.

Where your data goes

I built BLO VAULT so your balances and transactions are kept on your phone. Here is the whole path, including what I can see.

  1. 1

    Your bank, through Plaid

    You log in on Plaid's window or your bank's own page. BLO VAULT never sees or stores your bank password.

  2. 2

    The server passes it along

    Balances and transactions go through my server to your phone without being saved. The bank connection token is stored encrypted with a key on your phone (plus a one-way fingerprint used to confirm ownership).

  3. 3

    Your iPhone keeps the ledger

    Your transactions, balances and account names are stored on your phone in an AES-256 encrypted database. The key stays in the iOS Keychain. Face ID or your device passcode opens the app when one is set.

Backups. Backups are encrypted on your phone with a recovery key before they leave the phone. They go to your own iCloud by default, or to my server as encrypted data if iCloud isn't available. I can't read either copy.

Apple Card and Apple Cash. They connect through Apple on your iPhone with "Add Apple Card". No bank login. That data is read on your iPhone, and my server never receives it in readable form. Needs iOS 17.4 or later.

What I can see

  • Your sign-in email from Sign in with Apple (it can be a private relay address)
  • Which banks you've linked and how many accounts and institutions (for billing and support; the count can include Apple Card)
  • Your plan, trial status, app version and the last time the app was opened
  • Your push token, if you allow notifications
  • Your bank-linking attempts (outcome, error code, institution)
  • Automatic reports when a bank connection fails (error code, the bank's Plaid institution ID, app version), filed without you sending anything, so I can help
  • Your encrypted backup, only if iCloud isn't available (I can't read it)
  • Your purchase status, through Apple and RevenueCat
  • Anything you send in More > Send feedback: your text, an optional screenshot and email, your device model and app version, and, only if you turn on diagnostics, bank names with sync status
  • Crash reports, only if you turn them on (off by default, scrubbed of amounts and emails)

What I don't keep

  • Your bank or card passwords (I never see them)
  • Your balances, transactions, account names or numbers (they pass through my server without being saved)
  • Your Apple Card or Apple Cash data in readable form
  • A readable copy of your backups (I can't read the encrypted copy)

Read-only. BLO VAULT can't move money or make payments.

No ads, no data selling. I never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Questions

Can you see my bank password?

No. You log in on Plaid's window or your bank's own page. BLO VAULT never sees or stores your bank password.

Can you see my transactions?

They pass through my server on the way to your phone, without being saved. On your phone they're stored in an encrypted database, and backups are encrypted before they leave the phone. The "What I can see" list above shows everything I do keep.

Is BLO VAULT a bank?

No. It's a budget app that tracks your spending and cash flow. The connection is read-only, so it can't move money.

Which accounts work?

Many US banks and cards through Plaid; some institutions aren't available. Apple Card and Apple Cash connect through Apple with "Add Apple Card" (iOS 17.4 or later). iPhone only for now.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting the app removes the app's database, but not everything. Your bank connections, your iCloud backup, your account and keys saved in the iOS Keychain stay. Deleting the app does not end your 14-day trial clock either. To remove everything, go to More > Settings > Delete account permanently before you delete the app. It removes your server backup right away, disconnects your banks by month end, then deletes your account. Deleting the app also doesn't cancel a subscription; cancel that in your App Store settings.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. 14 days, no purchase needed, with up to 3 linked institutions. After that, bank syncing needs BLO Pro at $5.99/month.

How does the low-balance heads-up work?

BLO VAULT looks at your bank balance and the bills and card payments it expects before payday. If one could put checking below zero, it schedules a notification up to three days ahead. It's an estimate, updated each time you open the app. You can turn it off in More > Message Center > Notifications.
