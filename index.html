What it does

Cash Flow runway

Your balance today, then every bill, card payment and paycheck ahead, period by period. With more than one account, BLO VAULT shows each account on its own line and tells you which one runs short, and when.

Ledger, budget and recurring

Every transaction in one ledger with categories. Budget pace shows whether a category is ahead of plan; Recurring collects subscriptions and bills; the Money calendar puts paydays and card due dates on one view.

Net worth and investments

Cash, cards, brokerage, employee stock plans, 401(k) and IRA, mortgage and home equity in one place, with daily history. A retirement planner runs thousands of market scenarios to show a likely range, not a single guess.

Your accounts, your way

Banks, cards and brokerages connect through Plaid. Apple Card and Apple Cash connect through Apple's FinanceKit on your iPhone. Receipt scanning and the optional assistant run on the device.

Where your data goes

We built BLO VAULT so that we never hold a readable copy of your finances. Here is the whole path your data takes.

  1. Step 1

    Your bank, through Plaid

    You sign in inside Plaid's secure window, or on your bank's own page. Your bank password goes to Plaid or your bank. BLO VAULT never sees it.

    Encrypted in transit (HTTPS)
  2. Step 2

    Our server relays, it doesn't store

    Balances and transactions pass through to your phone without being saved. The bank-link token is stored only as ciphertext that needs a key kept on your phone.

    Encrypted in transit (HTTPS)
  3. Step 3

    Your iPhone is the vault

    Your ledger is saved in an AES-256 encrypted database. The key sits in the iOS Keychain, and Face ID or your passcode opens the app. All calculations run here.

Backup: locked before it leaves

Backups are encrypted on your phone (AES-256-GCM) with a recovery key only you hold, then stored in your own iCloud. Apple Card and Apple Cash data, read through Apple's FinanceKit, stays on your phone.

What we can see

  • The sign-in address from Sign in with Apple (it can be a private relay address)
  • Which institutions you've linked and how many accounts, for billing
  • Your subscription or beta status

What we can't see

  • Your bank or card passwords
  • Balances, transactions, account names or numbers
  • Your backups
  • Read-only: BLO VAULT can't move money or make payments.
  • Automated security checks run every week.
  • Panic Delete wipes everything on the device in one step and removes your bank connections.
  • The optional assistant runs on-device; your ledger is not sent to cloud AI services.
  • We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who builds BLO VAULT

BLO VAULT Team. It started with one working engineer who uses AI every day at work and wanted the same freedom with personal finances, without handing them to someone else's server.

So we flipped the usual design. Instead of a cloud service that stores your money data, BLO VAULT only fetches your financial data through Plaid and does every calculation on your iPhone. Modern iPhones are powerful enough to run it all on the device, and their built-in hardware security, the Keychain and Face ID, guards the encrypted ledger.

Your balances and transactions are never stored outside your phone.

Questions

Can you see my transactions?

No. Transactions travel from Plaid through our server to your phone without being saved on the way, and are stored only on your phone, encrypted.

Which banks work?

Most U.S. banks, card issuers and brokerages supported by Plaid, plus Apple Card and Apple Cash on iPhone.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. The beta is iPhone only.

What happens if I stop using it?

Your bank connections are removed when your trial, subscription or beta access ends. Data on your phone stays until you delete it, and Panic Delete erases it immediately.
