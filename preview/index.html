See exactly what my server keeps

  1. 1. Your bank, through Plaid

    You log in on Plaid's window or your bank's own page. BLO VAULT never sees or stores your bank password.

  2. 2. The server passes it along

    Balances and transactions go through my server to your phone without being saved. The bank connection token is stored encrypted with a key on your phone (plus a one-way fingerprint used to confirm ownership).

  3. 3. Your iPhone keeps the ledger

    Your transactions, balances and account names are stored on your phone in an AES-256 encrypted database. The key stays in the iOS Keychain. Face ID or your device passcode opens the app when one is set.

Backups. Backups are encrypted on your phone with a recovery key before they leave the phone. They go to your own iCloud by default, or to my server as encrypted data if iCloud isn't available. I can't read either copy.

Apple Card and Apple Cash. They connect through Apple on your iPhone with "Add Apple Card". No bank login. That data is read on your iPhone, and my server never receives it in readable form. Needs iOS 17.4 or later.

What I can see

  • Your sign-in email from Sign in with Apple (it can be a private relay address)
  • Which banks you've linked and how many accounts and institutions (for billing and support; the count can include Apple Card)
  • Your plan, trial status, app version and the last time the app was opened
  • Your push token, if you allow notifications
  • Your bank-linking attempts (outcome, error code, institution)
  • Automatic reports when a bank connection fails (error code, the bank's Plaid institution ID, app version), filed without you sending anything, so I can help
  • Your encrypted backup, only if iCloud isn't available (I can't read it)
  • Your purchase status, through Apple and RevenueCat
  • Anything you send in More > Send feedback: your text, an optional screenshot and email, your device model and app version, and, only if you turn on diagnostics, bank names with sync status
  • Crash reports, only if you turn them on (off by default, scrubbed of amounts and emails)

What I don't keep

  • Your bank or card passwords (I never see them)
  • Your balances, transactions, account names or numbers (they pass through my server without being saved)
  • Your Apple Card or Apple Cash data in readable form
  • A readable copy of your backups (I can't read the encrypted copy)

Delete from inside the app. More > Settings > Delete account permanently removes your server backup right away, disconnects your banks by month end, then deletes your account. Erase this phone wipes this iPhone's data at once and schedules your account and bank connections for removal at month end. Sign back in before month end to restore.

It's a budget app. BLO VAULT tracks spending and cash flow. It isn't a bank and doesn't give financial advice.

Questions

Can you see my bank password?

No. You log in on Plaid's window or your bank's own page. BLO VAULT never sees or stores it.

Can you see my transactions?

They pass through my server to your phone without being saved. The full list of what I do keep is under "See exactly what my server keeps".

Which accounts work?

Many US banks and cards through Plaid; some aren't available. Apple Card and Apple Cash connect through Apple (iOS 17.4 or later).

Is BLO VAULT a bank?

No. It's a budget app, and the connection is read-only.

What happens if I delete the app?

Your bank connections, iCloud backup, account and trial clock stay, and a subscription isn't cancelled. To remove everything, use More > Settings > Delete account permanently first.
